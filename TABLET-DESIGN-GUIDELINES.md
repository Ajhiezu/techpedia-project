# Tablet Design Guidelines — Phase 2.6

- Use an 8-column layout and permit hero/about content to stack when copy becomes constrained.
- Use two-column grids for cards and preserve adequate line length.
- Collapse global navigation before the header crowds; do not depend on hover.
- Process may use a 3×2 grid before its mobile vertical sequence.
- Verify both portrait and landscape; controls may wrap without overlap.
