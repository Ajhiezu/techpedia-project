# About Us — High-Fidelity Design

## Artboard & Narrative Logic

About is an evaluation page built around identity, principles, and collaboration—not company scale. It uses an editorial, light-first layout with a strong narrative and modular process visual.

```text
HEADER
INTRO HERO: positioning story + abstract partnership/system visual
VISION & MISSION
BRAND VALUES: five cards
EXPERTISE: capability map (validated scope only)
DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY: narrative split
OUR PROCESS PREVIEW: six steps
FOUNDER / TEAM: conditional component
CTA
FOOTER
```

## Section Specification

| Section | High-fidelity treatment | Content rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intro hero | White 7/12 narrative + 5/12 structured visual. H1 **A technology partner for practical business progress.** | Do not imply office, company age, team size, or geographic scale. |
| Vision & mission | Light Background, two-column composition; Vision in a larger statement card, Mission as five concise action items. | Uses approved Phase 1 statements. |
| Brand values | White, five value cards in 3+2 grid: Clarity, Ownership, Practical Innovation, Collaboration, Continuous Improvement. | Value descriptions remain behavior-led, not claims of external awards. |
| Expertise | Light Background capability map across web, commerce, systems, operations, UI/UX, support. | Show only actual/confirmed service scope; no technology badges until verified. |
| Development philosophy | White 6/6 split: business context → clear scope → purposeful build → continuous improvement. | Explains approach rather than guaranteeing outcomes. |
| Our Process preview | Six numbered steps with `Explore Our Process` route/anchor. | Matches Process design and service pages. |
| Founder / Team | Conditional portrait/profile grid; photo, name, role, short expertise. | Omit entirely until identity, role, image, and permission are confirmed. |
| CTA | Deep Blue band: **Let's discuss what your business needs next.** | `Start a Conversation` → Contact. |

## Visual Language

- The intro visual is a modular partnership map: business goal, discovery, solution, and improvement connected by restrained blue lines.
- Founder/team portraits, when allowed, use consistent editorial crop and neutral backdrop; never use stock portraits as team representation.
- The About navigation item is active; header/footer match all core pages.

## Responsive

- Desktop: hero and philosophy split 7/5 or 6/6; Values 3+2.
- Tablet: Values 2 columns; hero can retain split or stack based on narrative length.
- Mobile: narrative before visual; value cards single column; process is vertical; team section remains omitted until valid.
