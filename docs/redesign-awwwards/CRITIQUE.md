## Ranked fixes

1. **Finish the existing need-to-system idea across the process section.** `final/desktop-full.png` currently puts a generic six-step list beside one mostly static application frame. Make each active step reveal its corresponding real artifact or annotated application detail, preserving the calm reduced-motion version. This would lift Creativity and Design more than another decorative effect.
2. **Recover cold mobile first paint.** Investigate why `mobile.vitals.lcpElement` is `p.lead` at 4,924 ms; make the lead and headline readable before optional reveal/motion work. Deliver appropriately sized modern project images, especially the 757 KB StayEase PNG. Repeat a fresh-cache 4× CPU/slow-4G run with LCP below 2.5 s.
3. **Demonstrate working transitions and complete interaction feedback.** Resolve the reported transition abort; record intermediate navigation frames. Give `h-01` a deliberate underline/indicator movement or fuller surface response. Recapture `h-02` and `h-03` around the actual visible targets before accepting or rejecting their hover behavior.
4. **Edit the mobile reading burden and image framing.** `final/mobile-full.png` repeats four lengthy project descriptions, six process explanations and four quote blocks. Keep a short problem/contribution summary visible and put extended detail behind existing disclosure/navigation paths. Remove the broad empty pale band inside the process screenshot frame (`r-02-mid.png`).
5. **Resolve portfolio status and evidence.** Replace repeated “status pengerjaan perlu dikonfirmasi” with verified, dated scope/status when available. For each project, distinguish the actual contribution and documented result from features and demo intent; do not invent metrics.
6. **Close the remaining measurable details.** Raise the 65×17 link hit area to at least 24 px high; repair the heading skips and figure role warnings; inspect the ten contrast-review items. Record explicit OG-image and 404 results and shorten overlong metadata. Keep the visible skip link behavior; correct its detector rather than assuming it is absent.
