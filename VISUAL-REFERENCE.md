# Visual Reference — Techpedia Project

## 1. Research Purpose

This research identifies transferable design principles from established technology brands. It does **not** authorize copying layout, copy, visual assets, interaction patterns, or identity. Observations were made from the official pages listed below during Phase 2.1 research.

## 2. Reference Analysis

| Reference | Relevant observed principle | Adaptation for Techpedia Project | Do not copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Thoughtworks](https://www.thoughtworks.com/about-us) | Business/technology positioning is communicated through editorial hierarchy and a clear explanation of capability. | Use narrative-led About and service intros that connect technology to business outcomes. | Their brand colors, imagery system, global scale claims, and impact statistics. |
| [Stripe](https://stripe.com/products) | Broad product offerings can be organised by use case and product area instead of a flat feature list. | Present services through business problems, use cases, and focused service cards. | Their purple gradient language, product UI, copy structure, and financial-product conventions. |
| [Linear](https://linear.app/homepage) | Focused messaging, product-forward visual storytelling, and clear rhythm make complex capability feel simple. | Use one strong message per hero and show process/interface visuals with a clear explanatory role. | Their dark visual identity, product screenshots, motion style, and customer proof. |
| [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) | Large-type hierarchy, clear product grouping, and confident restraint enable fast scanning. | Use concise headline systems, disciplined grids, and strong CTA hierarchy on solution pages. | Their black-and-white identity, product architecture visuals, wording, and platform claims. |

## 3. Synthesised Direction

The references suggest four useful principles for a corporate software agency:

1. **Make complexity scannable.** Use short hierarchy, service grouping, and predictable section patterns.
2. **Show capability, not decoration.** Interface-like visuals, process diagrams, and product context create more credibility than generic abstract art.
3. **Lead with a point of view.** A concise business-centric headline gives the page a clear reason to exist.
4. **Use restraint to signal quality.** Whitespace, typography, and interaction feedback can carry more authority than heavy visual effects.

Techpedia Project applies these principles through a light white-and-Royal-Blue system appropriate to its own positioning—not the dark, gradient, or product-platform identities of any reference.

## 4. Reference-to-Decision Map

| Techpedia decision | Basis from research | Phase 1 alignment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Editorial hero with one primary CTA | Scannability and confident hierarchy. | One primary action per section. |
| Services organised around needs/use cases | Product grouping makes choice simpler. | Services IA and target pain points. |
| Purposeful process/system visuals | Product-forward explanation makes intangible work concrete. | Wireframe visual direction and service detail structure. |
| Restrained surface, border, and motion system | Quality is communicated by precision. | Corporate Professional and motion guidelines. |
| No unverified social proof | Reference patterns are not a substitute for evidence. | Brand trust rules and content constraints. |
